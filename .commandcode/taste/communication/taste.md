# Communication Preferences

- Communicates in Chinese and wants user-facing text/menus in Chinese; when asking for AI prompts/skill content, often specifically wants an English version. Confidence: 0.85
- Frequently asks for generated commands/prompts to be copied directly to the clipboard ("直接复制到我的剪切板"). Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers diagnosing by pasting concrete logs/commands and pointing to specific servers/devices rather than generic descriptions. Confidence: 0.7
- Reports bugs as numbered lists of observed symptoms (including which UI evidence contradicts the seeming conclusion, e.g. "stop button is still showing so it likely hasn't finished") and asks for root-cause investigation ("排查一下…看看是为啥") rather than an immediate fix. Confidence: 0.6
