Manage background jobs started by the shell or task tools.

Actions:
- `list`: show jobs and their current states
- `status`: show one job's state and latest output
- `logs`: show one job's latest captured output
- `wait`: make a short wait for one job to finish (1s by default, 5s maximum)
- `stop`: stop one job and its managed process

Do not repeatedly poll a running job or use it as a long foreground wait. Continue independent work and rely on the completion message added to the conversation.
